A few practical notes

Tolls run homeward. Golden Gate, Bay, San Mateo and Dumbarton all charge in one direction only, and it's the one that brings you back. Roughly $7 for the East Bay, $9–11 for Marin.

Call ahead on the small ones. Lick Observatory, the Nike Missile Site, Point Bonita, the Bay Model, di Rosa and Hakone all keep limited days. Muir Woods, Big Basin and Año Nuevo need reservations.

Napa is here without the wine. It appears for its sculpture park, public market and river walk. Every lunch suggestion on this page works fine without a drinks order.
